ANATOMY 3D STUDY

Support

Need help with Anatomy 3D Study on iPhone or iPad? Contact hello@vomcore.com.

Study the two limb lessons

Choose the right upper or lower limb lesson. Drag to rotate the 3D model, pinch to zoom, and tap a bone to see its name and function card. Hide labels when you want to test your recall, then revisit structures you missed. The first release includes ten bones and works offline without an account.

If something does not work

Close and reopen the app, then try the lesson again. If the problem continues, tell us which lesson and structure were open, what you expected, and what happened. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the app version. A screenshot can help. Do not send personal health information.

Privacy and content

Study progress stays on your device. See the privacy policy for details. The app is an educational study aid and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.
