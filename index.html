ANATOMY 3D STUDY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Anatomy 3D Study is provided by Volkan Günay (Vomcore). Questions: hello@vomcore.com.

What the app stores

The app keeps your answer count, correct answer count, missed structures and completed study session count in local storage on your device. It uses these values to show your study progress, offer a review of missed structures and time an optional Apple rating request. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Data collection

The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, crash reporting SDK or server. We do not collect your study answers or personal information, and we do not track you across apps or websites. The anatomical models and lesson text are bundled with the app.

External links and App Store

The model credits screen links to the BodyParts3D source and license. That site opens only when you tap the link and is governed by its own privacy policy. If you choose to rate the app, Apple handles that action under its own terms and privacy policy.

Health information

This app is a study aid. It does not ask for, store or transmit information about your health and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

Children and changes

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated before the relevant version is released.
